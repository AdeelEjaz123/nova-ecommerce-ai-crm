# Delivery Issues, Delays & Resolution Guidelines

> **Knowledge Base Metadata:**
> - **Document Title:** `Delivery Issues, Delays & Resolution Guidelines`
> - **Category:** `shipping`

---

## 1. Delayed Shipments
Standard domestic shipping takes **2 to 4 business days**. If a shipment is delayed beyond **5 business days** due to weather, holiday volume, or carrier congestion:
- Our logistics team automatically opens a priority trace with FedEx/UPS.
- An automatic **$15 shipping credit** is refunded directly to your original payment method.
- Customers can request a real-time status update at any time by quoting their **Order ID** to our AI assistant or support team.

---

## 2. Lost or Stolen Packages
If carrier tracking shows **no movement for 48 consecutive hours**, or if tracking states **"Delivered"** but you have not received your package:
1. Please check with neighbors, household members, and your building's reception or mailroom.
2. If the package is still missing after 24 hours of delivery confirmation, contact our care team at `support@novacommerce.com` or via the live AI assistant.
3. NOVA will immediately dispatch a **free priority replacement shipment** or issue a **100% full refund via Stripe** with zero deduction.

---

## 3. Damaged Items on Arrival
If your package arrives with physical damage, broken box seals, or defective components:
- Take a clear photo of the damaged outer box and product.
- Report the issue within **7 days of delivery**.
- Our team will instantly issue a prepaid return shipping label and dispatch a brand-new replacement unit within **24 hours**.

---

## 4. Incorrect Shipping Address & Rerouting
- **Before Dispatch (Within 2 Hours):** Shipping addresses can be modified completely free of charge within 2 hours of placing the order before warehouse dispatch.
- **In Transit:** Once an order has been picked up by FedEx/UPS, address rerouting requests require a standard **$10 carrier intercept fee**.

---

## 5. Failed Delivery Attempts
- Carriers will make up to **3 delivery attempts** to your specified address.
- If you are unavailable on all 3 attempts, the courier will leave a notice card and hold the package at your **nearest local courier depot or post office for 5 business days** for self-pickup.
- If uncollected after 5 business days, the package will be returned to our central distribution center.

---

## 6. Customs & International Duties
- All international import duties, VAT, and customs fees are **100% prepaid by NOVA at checkout**.
- Customers will never be asked to pay surprise duties on arrival.
- If a package is temporarily held in international customs for documentation, email `customs@novacommerce.com` and our customs broker will clear it directly.
